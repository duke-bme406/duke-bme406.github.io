Class Ordering App

Every purchase for your team project goes through this app. You request an item, the teaching lab members approve and place the order, and you get a notification when it arrives. Your team's budget updates as you go.

Open the ordering app Bookmark this page or the app itself. Both always point to the current version.
1. Sign in 2. Check your budget 3. Request an item 4. Track it 5. When it arrives Help

1Sign in and get approved

  1. Open the app and click Sign in with Google to log in with your Google account.
  2. The first time, you will see a Request access screen. Enter your full name. If the teaching lab members gave you an access code, enter it too, and you will be let in right away.
  3. Click Submit request. Without a code you will see Waiting for admin approval. The teaching lab members will approve you and assign you to your team. Come back later and click Check again.
If you have more than one Google account or browser profile, you can only access the app from the one you signed up with. Open the app from that profile each time.

2Check your team budget first

Click My Budget in the top bar. It shows your team's total budget, how much has been spent, and what is left.

If an order would put your team over budget, the app may stop you from submitting it. Talk to your TA before that happens rather than after.

Before ordering something new, search the Catalog. It lists items the lab and class have bought before, with vendor, catalog number and last price, so you can reuse a known good product instead of hunting for one.

3Request an item

Click Add Request at the top of the left sidebar and fill in the form. Fields marked with a red star are required.

FieldWhat to put there
Catalog #The vendor's catalog or part number. If the item is already in the catalog, typing it fills in most of the form for you.
Product URLA direct link to the exact product page. Always include one. Use the vendor's page if there is no item link.
Vendor and Item NameWho sells it and what it is. Put the actual company, not a marketplace.
Unit / pack size and QuantityWhat one unit is (for example "pack of 100") and how many units you need at that price.
Type and SubcategoryPick the closest match from the lists. This is how spending is reported.
Grant IDYour team. It is filled in for you once the teaching lab members have assigned you to a team.
JustificationOne or two sentences on what this is for in your project. Be specific, because approvers read it.
AttachmentsOptional. Add a quote or required form if you have one.

Click submit when you are done. If your project needs custom oligos or gene fragments and you see a DNA order button under Add Request, use that instead of the regular form.

Submitting a request does not buy anything. The teaching lab members review it and place the order for you. Never buy items yourself and ask to be reimbursed unless the teaching lab members tell you to.

4Track your order

The Orders view shows every request in one of four stages. Click any order to see its details, comments and history.

Needs ApprovalWaiting for a TA or instructor to approve it.
RequestedApproved and waiting to be ordered.
OrderedPurchased and on its way.
ReceivedArrived and logged.

Small orders may skip Needs Approval and go straight to Requested. You can cancel your own request while it is still waiting to be ordered. If the teaching lab members have a question, they will comment on the order or contact you.

5When it arrives

The teaching lab members normally unpack deliveries and mark items received in the app. When that happens, you get an email notification that your item has arrived and where it was put.

  1. Pick up your item and check that it matches what you ordered.
  2. If anything is missing or wrong, add a comment on the order right away.
  3. If a package ever comes to you directly, open the order in the Ordered stage and click Mark Received yourself so the teaching lab members know it arrived.

Help and common problems

I signed in but it says I am waiting for approval.

The teaching lab members have not approved you yet. Give it some time, then click Check again. If it has been more than a day, message your TA.

I cannot see my team's budget, or Grant ID is empty.

You have not been assigned to a team yet. Ask your TA to add you to your team in the app.

The app is blank or a button does nothing.

Reload the page with Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac or Ctrl+Shift+R on Windows. If you are signed into several Google accounts in one browser, try a private or incognito window and sign in with only your class account.

The app is slow the first time I open it.

That is normal. The first load of the day can take several seconds while Google starts the app.

I made a mistake in a request.

If it has not been ordered yet, cancel it and submit a corrected one, or add a comment explaining the change. Once it has been ordered, comment on the order and tell your TA.

Something looks broken or I have an idea for the app.

Click Feedback in the top right corner of the app and describe what happened. Include what you clicked and what you expected.
